# PPT Department 2 Review Checklist

## Style Fit

- The deck uses 16:9 widescreen.
- The deck clearly chooses Light Formal Mode, Dark Product Mode, or a deliberate mix.
- The color palette stays within red, navy/blue, neutral gray, and limited product accents.
- The title rule, bilingual title stack, and top-right logo treatment are consistent where used.
- Closing/cover slides are sparse and not overloaded.

## Typography

- Chinese text uses `微软雅黑` or a close equivalent.
- English labels and numbers use `Century Gothic` or a close equivalent.
- Chinese title and English subtitle have clear size contrast.
- Dense product pages keep labels readable and avoid tiny uncontrolled text.

## Structure

- The deck starts with context or urgency before architecture details.
- Product pages move from goal -> architecture -> capability -> value -> roadmap.
- Proposal pages move from pain -> solution -> system -> value -> implementation.
- Appendix-level detail does not interrupt the main storyline.

## Slide Messages

- Each slide has a message title, not just a topic label.
- Red/blue emphasized words identify the real point.
- English subtitles are short and supportive, not full translations of every sentence.

## Visual Quality

- Diagrams align to a visible grid.
- Cards have consistent width, height, and spacing.
- Connector lines do not cross text.
- Screenshots and images are framed cleanly.
- No slide has random decorative icons or stock imagery.

## Data And Product Explanation

- Metrics include business meaning, not only numbers.
- Product capability pages connect function to operational value.
- Roadmaps include time, goal, key direction, and key capability.
- Diagnostic flows show closed-loop action, not only anomaly discovery.

## Final QA

- Render slides to images when possible.
- Check for text overflow, low contrast, inconsistent margins, and alignment drift.
- Search for placeholder text, old source names, and leftover unused labels.
- Confirm the deck can be scanned by a leader in under five minutes.
