# Source Observations

These observations were abstracted from the two source decks named in `SKILL.md`. Use this file to calibrate style decisions.

## Shared Foundation

- Both decks use 16:9 widescreen, 13.33 x 7.5 in.
- Both use a light off-white cover with a concise title, small English subtitle, and bottom slogan.
- Both rely on red emphasis plus deep blue/navy structure.
- Both use `微软雅黑` for Chinese and `Century Gothic` for English/numerals.
- Both use a small top-right logo on most content pages.
- Both use a thin horizontal rule under the title area.
- Both use bilingual title language on many slides.

## BGMH Deck Signals

Observed style:

- More formal, enterprise/government-facing.
- Dominant colors: `C00000`, `002060`, `0070C0`, `FFFFFF`, `F8F9FA`.
- Frequent use of logistics/port/vehicle imagery.
- Page types include: sparse thesis page, one-stop multimodal solution, system framework, service system, AI risk identification, roadmap and comparison pages.
- Repeated title structure: Chinese title with selected red/blue keywords and smaller English subtitle.
- Content often uses process diagrams, system layers, tables, and icon-like technical illustrations.

Use this direction when the deck needs authority, policy fit, logistics credibility, or management-level clarity.

## Cactus Deck Signals

Observed style:

- More product-and-data-platform oriented.
- Dominant colors: `000000`, `202631`, `C51516`, `06195B`, `315BD6`, `197B65`, `5B9B42`, `DFA21B`.
- More dense cards, timelines, product screenshots, roadmap matrices, and capability workflows.
- Page types include: product evolution, why now, construction goals, architecture diagram, dual-mode analysis, diagnostic loop, solution library, scene card assembly, value summary, roadmap, closing statement.
- English subtitles are compact and often technical: `Product Architecture Diagram`, `Dual-mode analysis approach`, `Solution Library Accumulation`.
- Phase labels and large numbers are prominent.

Use this direction when the deck explains product logic, data intelligence, platform capability, or internal delivery value.

## Extracted Quantitative Signals

- BGMH source: 22 slides.
- Cactus source: 12 slides.
- Common font sizes observed:
  - BGMH: 24 pt and 28 pt for many prominent text elements; 16 pt for body; 10-14 pt for detail.
  - Cactus: 22.4 pt for many headings; 8-15 pt for dense product details; 22-32 pt for large phase or value numbers.
- BGMH uses more pictures and OLE/table content.
- Cactus uses many auto-shapes, cards, lines, and structured blocks.

## Style Synthesis

When combining the two:

- Start with the BGMH light formal cover and title system.
- Use BGMH's calm red-blue hierarchy for executive and proposal pages.
- Borrow Cactus's card/timeline/diagnostic patterns for product and data pages.
- Keep closing slides sparse with one memorable sentence.
- Do not mix too many Cactus category colors into BGMH-style formal pages.
