# PPT Department 2 Design System

## Style Positioning

Department-2 PPTs should feel like a serious technology company explaining complex logistics, port, AI, and data-platform topics to internal leaders or enterprise stakeholders.

The style is not decorative. It relies on strong structure, restrained colors, precise diagrams, bilingual labeling, and repeated small brand signals.

## Visual Modes

### Light Formal Mode

Use for government/enterprise-facing pages, strategy pages, logistics hub concepts, and executive summaries.

Characteristics:

- Warm light background: `F8F9FA`, very light gray, or off-white.
- Red and deep blue emphasis.
- Sparse page composition with centered title pages or structured diagrams.
- Thin horizontal rules near titles.
- Small Westwell-style logo position at top right when brand assets are available.
- Industrial, logistics, port, vehicle, or map imagery used as evidence, not decoration.

### Dark Product Mode

Use for product interface, data intelligence, diagnostic workflows, architecture close-ups, and roadmap pages.

Characteristics:

- Deep charcoal or black system panels: `000000`, `0A0A0A`, `1D1D1F`, `202631`, `222831`.
- White text and cool gray dividers.
- Blue, green, amber, purple, and red accents for categories or status states.
- Dense but organized card systems, timeline grids, and capability matrices.
- Product screenshots or UI fragments embedded in structured frames.

Use dark pages selectively. Do not turn the whole deck dark unless the user explicitly wants a product-demo style.

## Color Palette

Primary neutrals:

- Background light: `F8F9FA`, `FFFFFF`, `F4F5F2`.
- Text dark: `000000`, `1D1D1F`, `202631`, `222831`.
- Secondary gray: `4B4B4B`, `536170`, `666666`, `9B9B9B`, `D7DDE8`, `E3E7EF`.

Brand and emphasis colors:

- Department red: `C00000`, `C51516`, `EE4048`.
- Corporate navy: `002060`, `06195B`, `07195A`, `0B346E`.
- Technical blue: `0070C0`, `315BD6`, `3158E8`, `416EEA`.
- Data green: `197B65`, `1E6B43`, `2EA165`, `5B9B42`.
- Scenario amber: `DFA21B`, `B84A45`.
- Auxiliary teal/purple: `2B9C9B`, `7543C9`.

Rules:

- Use red for key conclusions, risk, phase headers, or final emphasis.
- Use navy for structure blocks, system modules, and stable authority.
- Use blue/green/amber/purple only when encoding categories in product or data pages.
- Avoid large gradients and decorative color washes.
- Keep most pages to 1-2 accent colors plus neutrals.

## Typography

Preferred fonts:

- Chinese: `微软雅黑`.
- English and numerals: `Century Gothic`.
- Fallback: `Arial` for English-heavy pages.

Hierarchy:

- Cover title: 24-32 pt, bold, red or dark navy.
- Main slide title: 18-24 pt, bold. Use red for the key Chinese phrase and dark/navy for the rest when helpful.
- English subtitle: 7-11 pt, light gray or red, often directly under the Chinese title.
- Body: 9-15 pt depending on density.
- Labels and footnotes: 7-9 pt.
- Big numbers or phase labels: 22-32 pt.

Writing pattern:

- Use Chinese main titles with short English subtitles.
- Use title punctuation like `——` for product capability pages.
- Use short English labels such as `Why Now`, `Product Architecture Diagram`, `Solution Library Accumulation`.
- Do not overuse slogans. Keep `Make a Well Change.` for cover/closing or very sparse divider pages only.

## Layout And Spacing

Canvas:

- Use 16:9 widescreen, 13.33 x 7.5 in.
- Keep generous margins on formal pages.
- Use dense grid layouts only on product-system pages.

Common structure:

- Top-left title block.
- Thin title rule under the title area, often red or dark gray.
- Optional top-right logo.
- Main content area occupies the center 75-85% of the canvas.
- Bottom caption/source text should be subtle and aligned.

Spacing:

- Formal pages should breathe: large blank areas are acceptable.
- Product pages may be dense, but every card needs visible separation.
- Align card edges, timeline columns, and table cells precisely.

## Motifs And Components

Use these recurring motifs:

- Thin horizontal title rule.
- Red keyword emphasis inside a dark or gray title.
- Bilingual title/subtitle stack.
- Numbered phase blocks: `01`, `02`, `03`.
- System-layer bars: `System`, `AI`, `Data` or equivalent.
- Capability cards with heading, short explanation, and optional screenshot.
- Comparison cards for "internal / customer", "current / target", "problem / solution".
- Roadmap table with phases, timeline, goals, key direction, and key capability.
- Closing page with a single sentence and red-highlighted keyword.

Avoid:

- Generic rounded marketing cards unrelated to content.
- Large decorative icons with no information function.
- Random stock photos.
- Centered body paragraphs.
- Overly colorful pages where every category has equal weight.

## Image And Diagram Style

Image usage:

- Prefer port, logistics, vehicle, control tower, warehouse, system UI, or map imagery.
- Use images as proof or scenario context.
- Use screenshot panels when explaining product capability.
- Avoid dark, blurred, atmospheric imagery unless it is a section opener.

Diagram usage:

- Use flow arrows, layered architecture blocks, hub-and-spoke relationship diagrams, and timeline grids.
- Keep connector lines thin and aligned.
- Use red only for the core path or key risk/constraint.
- Use navy/blue for stable system modules.
- Use gray for supporting modules.

## Tables And Charts

Tables:

- Use light borders and clear row/column hierarchy.
- Align text left and numbers right.
- Use color bands only for phases, categories, or status states.
- Use dense tables for roadmap or comparison pages, not for raw data dumps.

Charts:

- Put the insight in the title, not just above the chart.
- Use direct labels where possible.
- Use red for deviation or warning, blue/green for normal categories.
- Keep gridlines subtle.

## Tone

Preferred tone:

- Structured, confident, analytical.
- Technical but readable.
- Specific nouns and verbs.

Avoid:

- Empty slogans such as "全面赋能", "打造生态闭环" unless required by source content.
- Unexplained buzzwords.
- Long paragraphs without visual grouping.
