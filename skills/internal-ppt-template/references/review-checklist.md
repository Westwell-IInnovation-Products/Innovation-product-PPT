# Internal PPT Review Checklist

Use this checklist before delivering an internal deck or template.

## Story

- The audience and decision goal are clear.
- The executive summary can stand alone.
- Slide order follows context -> insight -> recommendation -> action.
- Appendix slides contain details that would interrupt the main discussion.

## Slide Messages

- Every slide title states a conclusion or useful message.
- No slide depends on the presenter to explain its basic point.
- Repeated ideas are consolidated.
- Claims are supported by visible evidence or marked as assumptions.

## Visual Hierarchy

- The most important element is visually dominant.
- Each slide has one primary message.
- Dense slides are split or converted into structured tables, charts, or diagrams.
- Alignment, margins, and spacing are consistent.

## Data

- Charts have clear labels, units, date ranges, and sources.
- KPI definitions are visible or easy to find.
- Comparisons use consistent baselines.
- Color is used to communicate meaning, not decoration.

## Template Consistency

- Typography hierarchy is consistent across pages.
- Colors follow the selected internal palette.
- Icons and status tags use one style.
- Page numbers, source notes, dates, and confidentiality labels are consistent when used.

## Meeting Usefulness

- Required decisions are explicit.
- Risks and dependencies are visible.
- Next steps include owner and timing.
- The deck can be read quickly by a stakeholder who missed the meeting.
